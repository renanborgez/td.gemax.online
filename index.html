What's inside

Tactical depth without the bloat

Designed as a clean, replayable tower defense. No timers blocking play, no mandatory ads to win.

01

Authored paths, fixed waves

Designer-built routes you learn over runs. No procedural mush — every mission has a shape worth mastering.

02

Per-match difficulty

Easy, Normal, Hard, Insane. Pick your slope every run. Chapters ramp on top with a soft cap so late-game stays fair.

03

Tech-tree meta-progression

Earn shards from runs, spend them on a permanent tech tree. Effects are observable in-match — no invisible buffs.

04

Deterministic engine

Fixed-timestep simulation with a seeded RNG. Same seed, same run — useful for daily challenges and replays.

05

Skia rendering

GPU-accelerated 2D with glow shaders and clean vector primitives. Looks sharp on every density.

06

Plays offline

Single-player and offline-first. Save state lives on your device. No account required to play.

Loop

One run, three layers

In a mission

  • Tap a build cell to place a defense program
  • Upgrade in place — see the stat delta before you spend
  • Switch speed (1× / 2× / 3×) on the HUD when waves stabilize
  • Pause anytime; the engine clamps real time so background stalls don't punish you
  • Lose a life when an intrusion leaks; lose the run when lives hit zero

Between missions

  • Convert run shards into permanent tech-tree nodes
  • Unlock new towers and tags through chapter milestones
  • Replay any cleared mission on a higher difficulty for more shards
  • Stats and selections persist across devices via local save (cloud sync planned)

Monetization

How GeMax TD makes money

Up-front: the base game is free with optional purchases and ads. Below is exactly what you'll see and what you can opt out of.

What's purchasable

  • Cosmetic items — alternate tower skins, HUD themes, board palettes. Pure vanity, no gameplay effect.
  • Currency packs — one-time purchases of in-game shards used to accelerate the tech tree.
  • Netrunner Pass (subscription) — optional monthly subscription that grants a daily shard bonus, an extra loadout slot, and removes interstitial ads. Auto-renews; cancellable anytime in your store account.

Ads

  • Rewarded video — opt-in only. Watch a video to earn a shard bonus or a continue.
  • Interstitial ads — shown sparingly between missions. Removed by Netrunner Pass.
  • No ads target children. No ads inside an active mission.

Full details, refund policy pointers, and subscription terms are in the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Common questions

Is GeMax TD free?

Yes. The base game is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases and an optional subscription are available, and rewarded video ads are opt-in.

Does it require an internet connection?

No. Missions, the tech tree, and the save state run fully on-device. A connection is required only to download updates, watch rewarded ads, and verify in-app purchases.

Will my progress sync across devices?

At launch, save data is local. Cloud save is on the roadmap and will be opt-in.

Are purchases pay-to-win?

No. Currency packs only let you progress the tech tree faster — every node in that tree is reachable through normal play. Cosmetics have no gameplay effect.

How do I cancel the Netrunner Pass subscription?

Manage and cancel from your platform's subscription settings: iOS — Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Android — Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancellation stops the next renewal; current period continues until expiry.

What data does the game collect?

Crash logs, anonymous gameplay analytics, and (with consent) advertising identifiers used to serve ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in-app and at the OS level. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Is it suitable for kids?

The game is rated for a general audience but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account or made a purchase without consent, contact support@gemax.online.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Email support@gemax.online with your device, OS version, and a brief description. See Support for what to include.
